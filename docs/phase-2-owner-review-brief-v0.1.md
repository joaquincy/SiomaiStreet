# Siomai Street: business requirements for owner approval

**Version 0.1 · 27 October 2026 · Proposal only, not yet approved**

**Purpose:** Agree on the prices, application requirements and review process that the website, backend and admin app will follow. No website rules change until approved.

## 1. Packages and fees

The website currently shows conflicting prices. **Neither set is confirmed.**

| Package | Homepage price | Application price | Owner-approved price |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Food Cart / Starter | PHP 39,999 | PHP 150,000 | __________ |
| Kiosk | PHP 149,999 | PHP 300,000 | __________ |
| Store / Casual Dining | PHP 299,999 | PHP 500,000 | __________ |

**Proposal:** Use one package catalogue. Specify equipment, structure/fit-out, opening stock, packaging, training, software and support—including quantities and service limits. Confirm costs before setting prices.

List rent, deposits, permits, utilities, payroll, working capital, extra construction and delivery as exclusions unless expressly included. State taxes separately or clearly mark them included.

Propose free application and preliminary screening. Confirm royalties, marketing/software fees, supply costs, payment milestones, refunds, agreement term, renewal fees and territory rights. The FAQ currently says no monthly royalty; the app includes royalty tracking. Resolve this explicitly. Do not promise investment returns without evidence.

## 2. Eligibility and operating responsibilities

**Proposal:** Accept adult applicants aged 18+, beginners, joint applicants and properly represented businesses. Route foreign/entity eligibility questions for qualified review.

Applicants may apply without a site or prior employment. Require a realistic funding plan and an accountable operator. Passive investors need a manager plan; multi-unit applicants need a phased plan. Training and opening checks must be completed before operations begin.

Application approval does not automatically grant access to branch operations in the app.

## 3. Application fields

| Proposed initial requirements | Optional or conditional |
|---|---|
| Name, email confirmation, primary phone, city/province | Middle name and secondary phone optional |
| Applicant type, preferred region, format/package preference and timeline | Allow undecided package and no-site-yet paths |
| Funding estimate/source and operator plan | Financing, partner or manager details when applicable |
| Brief motivation and relevant experience | Employment history only if applicable; beginners need no invented jobs |
| Accuracy and no-guarantee acknowledgments; privacy notice | Separate authorization for specific verification checks |

**Collect later only when necessary:** full DOB/address, ID details, marital status, emergency contacts and photos. Co-applicants should confirm their own information and applicable permissions.

**Proposed essay simplification:** motivation and brand-values explanation 50–300 words each; personal-values answer optional, up to 200 words. Keep three selected brand values unless the owner changes this rule.

## 4. Documents

**Proposal:** Move sensitive evidence from mandatory initial intake to shortlisted verification.

| Stage | Documents |
|---|---|
| Initial application | Optional CV and site proposal/photos |
| Verification | Necessary ID, address and funding evidence; entity registration/representative authority where applicable; references only if justified |
| Onboarding | Reviewed agreements, applicable permits, site approval and training/opening records; photo only for a defined purpose |

Accept appropriate alternatives: bank certificate or relevant statement page for funds; utility bill, current lease or approved address certification for address. Require an ID back image only when needed.

**Proposed recency:** address/funds/site photos within 90 days; reference letter within 6 months; CV updated within 12 months; IDs/permits valid where expiry applies. Allow redaction of unrelated transactions, full account numbers and unnecessary personal details while preserving verification information.

Use private PDF/JPG/PNG uploads up to 10 MiB each. Never request passwords, bank logins or OTPs. Secure access and file checks must be implemented and tested.

## 5. Legal and privacy

Limit background checks to necessary identity, authority, experience and funding verification. More intrusive checks need separate justification and reviewed authorization.

Use an NDA only when confidential information is actually disclosed. Do not claim documents were signed when only checkboxes exist. Keep marketing consent separate. Identify the actual business/controller and privacy contact. Have the full agreement and privacy wording reviewed before publication.

## 6. Contacts and response targets

**Verify before publishing:** franchise and privacy email addresses, business phone/address, social accounts, office hours and responsible staff. Current displayed contacts are unverified.

Proposed working calendar: **Monday–Friday, 9AM–5PM, Philippine time, excluding public holidays.**

| Action | Proposed target—not yet a public promise |
|---|---|
| Submission receipt | Immediately after confirmed saving |
| Completeness check | Within 2 business days |
| Initial substantive response | Within 5 business days after completeness |
| Applicant corrections | 14 calendar days, extensions available |
| Final review | Within 10 business days after required verification is complete |
| Pending-case updates | Every 5 business days |
| Support | Acknowledge within 1 business day; substantive reply within 3 |

Opening dates depend on site, permits, agreement, equipment and training. Do not promise a universal 2–4-week launch.

## 7. Retention and access

**Proposed periods, subject to legal review and technical capability:**

| Data | Proposed handling |
|---|---|
| Unsubmitted drafts | Expire after 30 days without editing; provide Clear Draft |
| Inactive applications | Review at 60 days; notify before closure |
| Declined/withdrawn/closed application answers | Delete or anonymize 6 months after closure |
| Raw ID/funding/background evidence | Delete 90 days after verification decision unless a documented need applies |
| Approved applications | Transfer necessary data; remove duplicate intake copies within 90 days |
| Decision/consent evidence | Retain for 24 months after closure, subject to review |
| Agreements, financial and statutory records | Legal/finance must set the applicable schedule |

Include backups, exports, failed uploads and logs in the deletion policy. Allow documented preservation holds. Restrict sensitive records to authorized reviewers; no general Staff access or default use by the AI agent.

## 8. Responsibilities and approval

- **Owner:** approves business terms and final decision authority.
- **Assigned reviewer:** checks applications and requests corrections.
- **Authorized approver:** approves/declines with recorded reasons.
- **Privacy lead:** handles data requests and retention oversight.
- **Project team/classmate:** implements matching website, backend and app rules; verifies permissions and integration.

Record every decision, actor, timestamp and requirements version. Provide a correction/escalation route. Keep onboarding and account activation separate from application approval.
